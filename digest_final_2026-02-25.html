The Data Center Political Crisis

TiTV • Watch

  • Political Affordability Trumps Tech Progress: Data center buildouts face bipartisan opposition not due to anti-tech sentiment but because electricity cost increases for voters create political liability—Bradley Tusk's core insight that "no politician will sacrifice their career so Sam Altman can become a trillionaire" explains why permitting delays persist despite AI importance.
  • Energy Efficiency as Regulatory Arbitrage: The solution isn't lobbying for exceptions but building chips and cooling systems requiring dramatically less power/water per compute unit, enabling deployment without grid strain—Tusk advises investing in memory architectures, alternate compute paradigms, and on-site power generation (micro-nuclear, hydrogen) rather than fighting political headwinds.

Inside Anthropic's Rogue AI Research

TiTV • Watch

  • Fellowship Program as Safety Research Engine: Anthropic's safety team output is now majority-driven by external fellows (college-age and PhD students) working 4-6 months—revealing that frontier safety research doesn't require years of experience, just structured mentorship and focused project scope across six categories: security, AI control, scalable oversight, model internals, model organisms, and Chinese model evaluation.
  • Automated Vulnerability Reproduction as Scaling Law: Current process of manually recreating agent exploits (e.g., banking website phishing) to fine-tune models is too slow—research proposes automating conversion of wild-caught attacks into training data, enabling systematic hardening against emerging threats rather than reactive patching after each incident.

Leaders who fail make this mistake | Nicolai Tangen (CEO, $2T Fund)

The Knowledge Project • Watch

  • Arrogance as Terminal Leadership Flaw: Tangen observes failed leaders exhibit systematic pattern of believing they possess superior answers, dismissing contradictory evidence, and avoiding advisors who might change their minds—intellectual humility and genuine curiosity about being wrong differentiate sustained success from eventual failure.
  • Diversity as Decision Insurance: Effective leaders deliberately surround themselves with advisors who disagree and can win arguments through rigorous evidence, treating dissent as loyalty rather than threat—contrasts with failing leaders who optimize for comfort through yes-people and interpret disagreement as insubordination.

Why a 3x fund return is NOT enough

20VC • Watch

  • Fee Drag Erodes Nominal Returns: A 3x venture fund sounds strong but after 2% annual management fees over 10 years (20% of capital), 20-30% carry on profits, and illiquidity discount, LPs may achieve returns barely exceeding public market indices—explaining why institutional investors demand top-quartile performance (4-5x+) to justify allocation.
  • Power Law Math Requires Outlier Focus: Since venture returns concentrate in 1-2 winners per fund while 70% go bankrupt, managers must target 10-50x outcomes on winners to achieve portfolio-level 4-5x—driving industry behavior toward moonshot hunting rather than consistent moderate gains that work in traditional asset classes.
